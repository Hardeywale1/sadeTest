Sadé clickable prototype

DEPLOY TO NETLIFY
1. Upload the ZIP file in Netlify Drop, or unzip it and upload this folder.
2. Netlify should detect index.html automatically. No build command is required.
3. Test the main journey on a phone after deployment.

IMPORTANT
- This is a usability prototype, not a production health or telemedicine service.
- It does not submit forms, process real payments, book real appointments or store health data.
- Provider profiles, facilities, dates, availability and prices are illustrative.
- Keep the Netlify site private or password-protected during early testing where possible.
